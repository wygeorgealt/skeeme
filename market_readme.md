# What is Skeeme?

Skeeme is an AI-powered study assistant built specifically to solve the biggest problem students face: **knowing the answers vs. actually understanding them.**

We don't just give students the answer key. We give them the tutor, the practice test, and the revision cards—all in one seamless flow. By combining advanced OCR with a context-aware AI engine, Skeeme turns any textbook question, past paper, or class note into a personalized learning loop.

## The Core Features

### 1. Smart Scan (Calc vs. Theory Auto-Detection)
Most apps force students to choose between "Math Mode" and "Theory Mode." Skeeme is smarter.
Students just snap a photo of a question, and our AI automatically detects what kind of problem it is:
* **For Calculations (Math, Physics, Chemistry):** Skeeme generates a clean, numbered, step-by-step breakdown leading to the exact final answer.
* **For Theory (Biology, History, Literature):** Skeeme reads the prompt and generates a structured explanation using bullet points, clear paragraphs, and a one-line "Key Takeaway" summary. 

### 2. The Viral Study Loop ("Practice Similar")
Getting the answer is only step one. Skeeme is designed to build mastery. 
After a student scans a question and reads the solution, they can tap **"Practice Similar Questions."**
Skeeme instantly extracts the core topic from the scanned image and drops the student directly into the AI Quiz Generator—pre-filled and ready to go. One tap turns a moment of confusion into an active learning session.

### 3. AI Quiz Generator
Students can generate a full, custom quiz in 15 seconds by typing a topic or uploading a document (PDF, Word, or TXT).
* **Flexible Formats:** Choose between Multiple Choice, Theory/Essay questions, or a mixed format.
* **Granular Control:** Select the exact number of questions and the difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard).
* **Strict Timer Mode:** Simulate real exam pressure with a forced-submission timer.

### 4. Smart Grading Engine
Our AI doesn't just grade Multiple Choice. When a student types an essay or theory answer, Skeeme reads it like an actual teacher would. It awards partial credit, points out missing keywords, and explains exactly *why* a particular grade was given. 

### 5. Branded PDF Export
Every quiz completed in Skeeme can be exported as a beautiful, distraction-free PDF containing the original questions, the student's answers, the correct answers, and the AI's detailed explanations. 
Students can instantly share these PDFs to class WhatsApp groups or Telegram channels using the native iOS/Android share sheet. (And yes, every PDF contains a footer redirecting their classmates to download Skeeme).

### 6. AI Flashcards
Memorization made simple. Students can upload a 50-page lecture note PDF and say, *"Turn the key definitions in Chapter 3 into 20 flashcards."* Skeeme extracts the context, ignores the fluff, and generates a deck.

### 7. Offline Study History
Real learning happens over time. Every quiz taken, score earned, and question missed is saved directly to the device. 
* Review past mistakes without an internet connection.
* Track percentage scores over time to measure actual improvement.
* Background Sync ensures data is backed up to the cloud the moment the device reconnects.

## The Skeeme Advantage
We aren't building a cheating tool. We are building the ultimate revision companion. By linking **Scan → Understand → Practice → Export**, Skeeme creates a loop that not only helps the individual student master their curriculum but naturally spreads through student networks via shared PDFs.

**Study smarter. Not harder. Skeeme.**
