Privacy Policy

Skeeme values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information, study content, and usage data when you use the Skeeme app and website.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, profile details, and study activity. We may also collect content you upload, notes you create, AI prompts, and usage data necessary to provide the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to create and secure your account, deliver AI-powered study features, improve learning outcomes, respond to support requests, maintain the platform, and prevent misuse or abuse.

3. Your Study Content

Content you upload to Skeeme may be processed to generate quizzes, flashcards, explanations, and revision prompts. We do not sell your study content to third parties.

4. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage you to keep your login information private.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers for hosting, analytics, and payments. These providers are required to handle information in a secure and compliant manner and are subject to confidentiality obligations.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and standard analytics tools to understand how users interact with the Service, improve performance, and support product development.

7. Your Choices

You may update your account settings at any time, manage communication preferences, and request deletion of your account where permitted by applicable law.

8. Retention

We retain account and study data for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or on the website.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the support channels available in the Skeeme app or website.
