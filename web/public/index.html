A few things you might be wondering.

Quick answers before you start.

What can I do with Skeeme?

You can scan or upload study material, ask the AI tutor questions, and create summaries, quizzes, and flashcards to support revision.

Do I need to be preparing for a particular exam?

No specific exam is required. Skeeme is for students studying across subjects and working toward their own learning goals.

Where can I get the app?

The Android app is available on Google Play. Use the download button on this page to open its listing.
