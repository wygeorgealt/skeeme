## 3. Credit Usage Breakdown
How your credits are spent across different features:

| Feature | Credit Cost |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scan & Solve** | 2 (Base) + 4 per question solution |
| **Quiz Generation** | 1 per question + 5 per 500 words of source material |
| **Flashcard Deck** | 0.5 - 1.5 per card + 5 per 500 words |
| **Theory Grading** | Included in Quiz flow |

*   **Weight Cost:** For long documents (PDFs/Lectures), we charge a small "Weight Cost" of 5 credits per 500 words to cover the massive AI processing required.
*   **Minimums:** Most generations have a 10-credit floor to ensure AI quality.
