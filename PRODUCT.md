# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students who want help studying across subjects. The landing page should speak to students broadly, not only to exam candidates.

## Product Purpose

Skeeme is an AI-powered study companion that helps students understand material, practice actively, and organize their study sessions.

## Positioning

Skeeme brings students' study materials, AI explanations, practice, revision, and progress into one learning workflow.

## Operating Context

The public website introduces the mobile app. The primary call to action should take Android visitors to the Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.skeeme.app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The repository describes note scanning, AI tutoring, quiz and flashcard creation, summaries, study planning, and progress tracking.
- Study materials submitted to AI features are sent to AI providers to generate requested responses and are not used to train AI models.
- Skeeme sells subscriptions through Google Play and the Apple App Store; subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the app or through the store used to subscribe. Trial and renewal terms are shown in the purchase flow.
- The service is for users age 13 and older. Users below the age of majority where they live need parent or legal guardian permission and supervision.
- Do not imply support for a specific exam, claim outcomes, or present illustrative sample data as measured results unless verified.
- An Android listing URL is confirmed. No iOS listing URL has been provided.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Skeeme name and existing logo.
- Use the existing Skeeme blue as a meaningful part of the identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- The student app's routes and capabilities are documented in `student-app/README.md`.
- The website has a student app mockup at `web/public/images/student_app_mockup.png`; its sample screens and metrics are illustrative, not verified evidence.
- The repository contains no verified testimonials or outcome metrics for the landing page.

## Product Principles

- Help students move from material to active practice.
- Make the next useful study action easy to understand.
- Encourage understanding and consistency without promising academic outcomes.
